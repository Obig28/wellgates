Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about applying, rates, and how we work with you.

Many applications receive a decision within 24–48 hours after we have your documents. Timing depends on loan type and completeness of your information.

We use a soft inquiry for pre-qualification in many cases, which does not affect your score. A hard inquiry may occur if you proceed to a full application, as with most lenders.

We disclose origination or processing fees before you sign. Your loan estimate lists the APR, payment, and any fees in one place so you can compare apples to apples.

Many of our products allow early payoff with no prepayment penalty. Your loan agreement will state the terms that apply to your specific product.

You can reach our servicing team by phone or secure message through your account. We assign a point of contact for business clients when applicable.
